# VocabStream illustration credits

Each image's creator, license, source, lesson, exact word sense, accessible description and content hash are recorded in `manifest.json`. The catalog contains two separately licensed collections. Files are served locally; viewing a lesson does not contact an image provider.

## Teaching use

Images accompany definitions and examples. Clear concrete referents can be used for image-to-word practice. Relationship, quantity and process diagrams marked `supporting` retain text-based assessment, then appear in answer feedback. Japanese descriptions provide equivalent information when an image fails or a learner uses assistive technology. Supporting diagrams may use short Japanese labels; they are not treated as standalone meaning questions.
